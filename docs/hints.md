# Hints

How the collection hints at animals you have not met yet. Hints describe an animal's habits; they never tell you what to do. A short, slow or late activity is never worse, so a hint is something to notice, not a task.

## When a hint shows

| Entry | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Shape species | "???" tile, no hint | Once you have met any entry of its base: a dark silhouette on the tile, and a habit line when tapped |
| Colour species | "???" tile, no hint | Once you have met its shape species: a silhouette, and "An uncommon coat of the Red fox" (12%) or "A very rare coat of the Red fox" (3%) |
| Base legend | "???" tile, no hint | Once you have met any entry of its base: a silhouette, and its riddle |
| Regional legend | Hidden outside its region | In its region: a silhouette and its riddle. Met: shown everywhere |

A hint never names the animal; the name stays "???" until you meet it. Tapping a tile with no hint shows "Not met yet. Anything can turn up on any outing."

## Habit lines (shape species)

A habit line is made from the base's home kinds and the species' strongest affinities: the kind phrase, then at most two affinity phrases, strongest first (loves before likes before avoids). A species with no affinities gets "Not fussy about when or how."

Example: Fennec (Fox, loves Fast, likes Day) → "Turns up on runs. Loves speed, fond of daylight."

### Kind phrases

| Home kind | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Run | Turns up on runs |
| Walk | Trots along on walks |
| Hike | Found out on hikes |
| Ride | Flies over rides |
| Swim | Paddles by on swims |
| Climb | Seen on climbs |
| Yoga, Workout | Joins yoga and workouts |
| Sleep | Comes while you sleep |

A base with two home kinds joins them with "and" (Squirrel: "Found out on hikes and climbs"). A species that is also home on another kind adds it (Kingfisher: "Flies over rides and swims").
